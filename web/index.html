    About TreeFinder

    NeurIPS 2025 · Datasets & Benchmarks Track

    TreeFinder: A US-Scale Benchmark Dataset for Individual Tree Mortality Monitoring Using High-Resolution Aerial Imagery

    Zhihao Wang, Cooper Li, Ruichen Wang, Lei Ma, George Hurtt, Xiaowei Jia, Gengchen Mai, Zhili Li, Yiqun Xie

    Paper (NeurIPS) Code (GitHub) Dataset (Kaggle) Dataset (Hugging Face)

    Dataset

    TreeFinder is the first high-resolution benchmark for mapping individual tree mortality across the contiguous United States. Dead trees were hand-annotated at the pixel level on 0.6 m NAIP aerial imagery, and the annotations were checked against multi-temporal NAIP and vegetation indices.

    • ~1,000 sites in 48 states
    • 23,000+ hectares
    • 20,000+ annotated dead trees
    • 0.6 m NAIP · R, G, B, NIR
    • Binary masks (1 = dead tree)
    • License: CC BY 4.0

    Benchmarks cover random, cross-region, cross-climate and cross-forest-type splits for CNN and vision-transformer segmentation models.

    This viewer

    Label polygons and masks are drawn from the dataset's annotation band over NAIP 2021–2023 mosaics from Microsoft Planetary Computer. Use the year switch to compare the labels against each NAIP acquisition. Viewer source: rcwang531/treefinder-web.
